The two pi-fancy-footer counters contributed by pi-agents now use the
footer's own naming style. They appear as `workflows` and `agents` in
`/fancy-footer` instead of `Active workflows` and `Agent progress`, so they
line up with built-in widget names like `branch`, `pr-ci`, and `cache-hit`.
