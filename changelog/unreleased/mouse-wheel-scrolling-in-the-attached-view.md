The mouse wheel now scrolls an attached agent's conversation in fullscreen mode,
the default. Point at the attached view and scroll as you would in Pi's own
transcript; Shift+↑ ↓ and Shift+PgUp/PgDn keep working.

In regular mode, the terminal keeps handling the mouse wheel, so use the
keyboard to scroll the attached view there.
